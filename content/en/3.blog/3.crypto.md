## Benefits

- P2P, no middlemen
- Personal keys, no Strong Identification
- Automated contracts and tokens
- Fee-less
- Censorship is very limited(visual)
- AI slop and genuine human creations separated
- Provides possibilities to live outside the system
- Depends of the amount of bravery, resources and networks
- User-created and curated content provides tokens
- Decentralized communities, physical place is not so important.
