## Idea

One of the best feelings in the world is being detached form artificial boundaries and letting the intuition lead the way.

Monetary concepts, learned behavior, blind trust and many hurdles on the way, but can be overcome with a hint of luck and some wits.

  :::div{icon="i-lucide-arrow-down" label="Top-down"}
  - Corporate interests
  - Political theatre
  - Perceived reality through the media
  - Self-censorship
  - Newspeak
  - Fear, intimidation
  :::

  :::div{icon="i-lucide-cat" label="Freedom"}
  - **Grassroots**
    Local or personal interests and resources
  - **Direct Decisions**
    Online polls, forms, information databases
  - **Reasoning**
    Thinking and following the gut feeling, figuring out the motives
  - **Open Communications**
    Expressing oneself truthfully with perhaps a hint of humour
  - **Logical Languages**
    Understanding the origins of a term and avoiding meaningless ones
  - **Love & Compassion**
    Thinking only works well in a certain environment and situations
  - **Feel**
    Emotions are not mental disorders, even people without real feelings claims so
