Cryptocurrencies have become more known the world of finance and economics. Based on decentralized blockchain technology, cryptocurrencies can replace the unreliable FIAT databases from the 70s. Direct trade, free transactions, and economic freedom.

![cat](catbirchdark.webp){.rounded-lg height="600" width="1000"}

## The Evolution of Cryptocurrencies

Bitcoin started around 2009, adopted by a bunch of people, who saw a future without third parties or blocked transactions, with a nice bonus called deflation. One person with own set of keys, censorship becomes impossible and identifications are outdated concepts.

As the needless electricity consumption with **PoW** proofing mechanism is starting to be outdated, there are a wide variety of options without extra fees or the need for official paperwork. The receipt itself can include text, pictures, videos and such, along with automated contracts designed for specific puropses. 

## 📈 Benefits

Cryptocurrencies present numerous technical advancements for everyone.

- **Financial Freedom** No need for confirmations from an authority, digital IDs, or other middlehand. Person to person, as most of the trade in the history has worked. Hard to beat a cost of 0.
- **Decentralized Finance:** DeFi is basically an alternative marketplace, where banks are not involved unless it is a hoax made by them. From basic financial services, like lending, borrowing, and trading, to advanced contracts and real life assets.
- **Contracts:** Cryptocurrency contracts can be made to validate, when certain actions are taken or certain rules have been followed. Imagination is the limit, technology is there.
- **Innovation:** When not hidered by artificial rules, people come up with the most amazing stuff. Why use over a 50 year old bank datasheet, which nobody understands anymore? 

## 📉 Challenges

Despite the big potential, cryptocurrencies also face challenges and risks, that should be considered.

- **Volatility:** The charts on trade prices go up and down all the time, the most known options are in control of Corporations and exchanges are mostly owned by banking institutions. Marketing speeches differ from reality greatly.
- **Regulatory Pressure:** Governments are trying to deceit people using only the currency they collect taxes with. As most people have no idea where money comes from, not to mention how it works, creating something out of nothing is still a valid way. No €, no problem.
- **Security:** Exchanges and various wallets are vulnerable to manipulation and theft, so having keys secured and not using third parties, keeps the coins safe. Passkeys and USB wallets are pretty good options.
- **Social Impact:** As the old currencies are failing by design, many laws and regulations are made to scare people off alternatives. Double and triple taxing is quite common in the countries with the most corrupt actors.

## Technogy

Applications built on top of blockchains provide a big benefit from just an centralized database with 1s and 0s, where currency is created out of thin air as debt.

One can make a new token system with some learning and doing, so communities can have their own means of exhcange with own values built-in.

Information age is here, so we need a way to store information, and cryptography with decentralized nodes seems like a no-brainer. Personal contracts instead of being controlled by an institution.
